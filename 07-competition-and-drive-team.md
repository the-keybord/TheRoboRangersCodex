# 07. Competition Protocol, Drive Team & Tournament Operations

---

## 🏎️ The Competition Philosophy

Tournaments are the ultimate celebration of the team's engineering efforts. On the field, in the pits, and in the stands, every RoboRanger member operates with discipline, composure, and Gracious Professionalism®.

## 🕹️ Drive Team Structure & Selection

The Drive Team is the operational unit that pilots the robot in official tournament matches.

```text
                │                              │
 ┌──────────────▼──────────────┐ ┌─────────────▼───────────────┐
 │        HUMAN PLAYER         │ │    DRIVE TEAM TECHNICIAN    │
 │ Game piece management & zone│ │ Cart handler, staging queue │
 └─────────────────────────────┘ └─────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Objective Selection Criteria
To eliminate favoritism, drive team slots are awarded based on an **objective, scored assessment**:

| Evaluation Component | Description | Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Game Rules Exam** | Written test covering Game Manuals, penalties, ranking points, and scoring zones. Must achieve $\ge 90\%$. | $25\%$ |
| **Timed Driving Trials** | Standardized obstacle course, cycle time test, and precision maneuver drills. | $35\%$ |
| **Composure & Communication** | Ability to listen to the Coach, remain calm under simulated failures, and avoid erratic driving. | $25\%$ |
| **Attendance & Reliability** | Verified attendance at build sessions and dedicated drive practice sessions. | $15\%$ |

### 2. Drive Team Backup / Alternates
A designated Alternate Driver and Technician must attend all drive practices and be ready to step in if a primary driver is ill or unable to compete.

## 🎪 Pit Operations & Pre-Match Checklists

The Competition Pit is a professional engineering workspace and our primary public exhibit.

### 1. The Pit Crew
* **Pit Captain**: Oversees pit organization, staging schedules, and judges' greeting.
* **Mechanical Specialist**: Inspects fasteners, chain tension, and structural integrity between matches.
* **Electrical & Battery Manager**: Charges, rotates, and logs battery voltages; checks wire strain relief.
* **Software Specialist**: Connects to the robot for log dumps, autonomous configuration, and sensor recalibration.

### 2. Pre-Match Checklist ("The 5-Minute Drill")
No robot leaves the pit for queuing without clearing the pre-match checklist:

- [ ] **Battery**: Fresh battery installed, securely strapped, reading $\ge 13.0\text{V}$ (under load).
- [ ] **Fasteners**: Motor mount set screws, wheel hubs, and slide clamps inspected with hex keys.
- [ ] **Wiring**: Main power switch tight; USB/Type-C and sensor cables zip-tied and strain-relieved.
- [ ] **Mechanisms**: Arms and slides placed in legal pre-match starting configuration (within sizing limits).
- [ ] **Controllers**: Gamepads plugged in, clean, and tested via controller diagnostics.
- [ ] **Pre-Load**: Proper starting game element loaded legally per game specifications.

## 📊 Scouting & Alliance Strategy

Championships are frequently won in the scouting stands. The RoboRangers field a dedicated **Scouting Corps**:

### 1. Match Scouting Protocol
* Every qualification match is observed by designated scouts tracking:
  * Autonomous points and starting positions.
  * Tele-op cycle counts and scoring locations.
  * Endgame reliability and penalties incurred.
  * Mechanical vulnerabilities observed (e.g., gets stuck on barriers, prone to tipping).

### 2. Alliance Selection & Pick Lists
* Prior to alliance selection, the Drive Coach, Scouting Lead, and Team Captain synthesize scouting data into a prioritized **Pick List**.
* Decisions are data-driven: compatibility with our autonomous path and complementary scoring speed take precedence over raw team reputation.
* Alliance discussions with prospective partners are conducted with complete transparency and respect.

## 🤝 Gracious Professionalism in Action

1. **Assisting Other Teams**: If an opposing team needs a motor, an Allen wrench, a 3D-printed part, or code troubleshooting, RoboRangers proactively assist them.
2. **Referees & Volunteers**: Referees and judges are unpaid volunteers. Only the designated Drive Coach (or Student Captain) may approach the question box to politely ask for clarification. Harassment or disputing calls aggressively is grounds for immediate removal from the drive team.
3. **Leaving the Venue Cleaner Than We Found It**: Before departing any event, RoboRangers conduct a complete clean-up of our pit space and surrounding aisles.
