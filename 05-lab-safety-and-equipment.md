# 05. Workshop Safety, Equipment & Battery Protocols

---

## ⚠️ The Safety-First Mandate

Safety is non-negotiable. The RoboRangers maintain an uncompromising commitment to personal well-being, workshop security, and responsible equipment operation. A momentary lapse in safety discipline can cause permanent injury or disqualify a team at competition.

---

## 🥽 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) & Dress Code

### 1. Mandatory PPE
* **Safety Glasses**: ANSI Z87.1 approved safety glasses must be worn **at all times** by every individual inside the workshop, testing arena, and competition pit area—regardless of whether they are actively operating tools.
* **Hearing Protection**: Required when running loud machinery (CNC routers, rotary tools, angle grinders).

### 2. Workshop Dress Code
* **Footwear**: Closed-toe, closed-heel shoes (rubber-soled work shoes or sneakers) are required. No sandals, crocs, or open footwear.
* **Hair**: Long hair must be tied securely back behind the neck.
* **Clothing**: Avoid baggy clothing, hoodies with dangling drawstrings, or loose ties that can catch in spinning chucks, gears, or belts.
* **Jewelry**: Necklaces, dangling earrings, rings, and wrist bracelets must be removed prior to working with power tools.

## 🛠️ Tool Certification Tiers

No student may operate a power tool without passing the corresponding practical evaluation administered by the Safety Officer or a Mentor.

```text
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ TIER 1: Unrestricted Tools                                  │
 │ Hand screwdrivers, hex keys, wrenches, pliers, wire         │
 │ strippers, calipers, hand files.                            │
 └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                ▼
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ TIER 2: Certified Power Tools (Requires Practical Checkoff) │
 │ Cordless hand drills, heat guns, Dremel / rotary tools,     │
 │ soldering iron, hot glue guns.                              │
 └──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┘
                                ▼
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ TIER 3: Heavy Machinery (Mentor Direct Supervision Only)    │
 │ Drill press, bandsaw, chop saw, CNC router, metal sheer.    │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Tool Guidelines
1. **Clamp Your Work**: Never hold raw metal or polycarbonate by hand while drilling or cutting; always secure workpieces in a vise or clamp.
2. **Never Force a Tool**: If a blade, bit, or endmill is smoking, chattering, or struggling, stop immediately and seek mentor inspection.
3. **3D Printing & Additive Manufacturing**:
   * No printing with toxic resins without dedicated fume ventilation.
   * Heated beds and hotends reach $>200^\circ\text{C}$; never touch nozzles while active.

## 🔋 Battery Safety & Charging Protocols

Batteries represent high-energy density hazards. Mishandled batteries can short-circuit, overheat, rupture, or cause electrical fires.

### 1. Charging Station Rules
* Batteries must **only** be charged using designated, approved smart chargers (e.g., REV Slim Battery Chargers or smart balance chargers).
* Never leave batteries charging unattended overnight. The last person leaving the workshop must ensure all chargers are disconnected.
* Charge only on non-flammable surfaces (metal tables, fireproof battery bags, or ceramic tile).

### 2. Pre-Use Inspection
Before installing a battery on the robot, verify:
- [ ] No cuts, cracks, abrasions, or exposed copper on wires.
- [ ] Tamiya / XT30 connector housing is tight and uncharred.
- [ ] Battery casing exhibits no swelling, leaking, or physical deformation.
- [ ] Voltage tested with a battery analyzer (discard or quarantine batteries reading below minimum cell thresholds).

### 3. Emergency Battery Incident Protocol
If a battery becomes hot to the touch, swells, or emits smoke:
1. **Evacuate the immediate area** and alert a mentor immediately.
2. Do **not** touch bare metal or breathe fumes.
3. If safe, use insulated tongs to transfer the battery into the **Fireproof Battery Quarantine Bucket** filled with dry sand.
4. Keep a Class C / ABC fire extinguisher accessible within 10 meters of the charging station.

## 🧹 The 15-Minute Workshop Sweep

A messy lab causes accidents and lost parts. Build sessions pause **15 minutes before adjournment** for mandatory team cleanup:

- [ ] All hand tools returned to their designated shadow boards or labeled drawers.
- [ ] Metal shavings, polycarbonate scraps, and zip-tie clippings swept from floors and tables.
- [ ] Soldering stations, heat guns, and power strips switched off and unplugged.
- [ ] Current robot chassis placed in the safe staging area with power switches verified OFF.
- [ ] Safety Officer and Team Captain inspect the room before anyone is dismissed.
