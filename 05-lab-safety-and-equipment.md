# 05. Workshop Safety, Equipment & Battery Protocols

---

## ⚠️ The Safety-First Mandate

Safety is non-negotiable. The RoboRangers maintain an uncompromising commitment to personal well-being, workshop security, and responsible equipment operation. A momentary lapse in safety discipline can cause permanent injury or disqualify a team at competition.

---

## 🥽 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) & Dress Code

### 1. Mandatory PPE
* **Safety Glasses**: ANSI Z87.1 approved safety glasses must be worn **at all times** by every individual inside the workshop, testing arena, and competition pit area—regardless of whether they are actively operating tools.
* **Hearing Protection**: Required when running loud machinery (CNC routers, rotary tools, angle grinders).

### 2. Workshop Dress Code
* **Footwear**: Closed-toe, closed-heel shoes (rubber-soled work shoes or sneakers) are required. No sandals, crocs, or open footwear.
* **Hair**: Long hair must be tied securely back behind the neck.
* **Clothing**: Avoid baggy clothing, hoodies with dangling drawstrings, or loose ties that can catch in spinning chucks, gears, or belts.
* **Jewelry**: Necklaces, dangling earrings, rings, and wrist bracelets must be removed prior to working with power tools.

```

### Tool Guidelines
1. **Clamp Your Work**: Never hold raw metal or polycarbonate by hand while drilling or cutting; always secure workpieces in a vise or clamp.
2. **Never Force a Tool**: If a blade, bit, or endmill is smoking, chattering, or struggling, stop immediately and seek mentor inspection.
3. **3D Printing & Additive Manufacturing**:
   * No printing with toxic resins without dedicated fume ventilation.
   * Heated beds and hotends reach $>200^\circ\text{C}$; never touch nozzles while active.

## 🔋 Battery Safety & Charging Protocols

Batteries represent high-energy density hazards. Mishandled batteries can short-circuit, overheat, rupture, or cause electrical fires.

### 1. Charging Station Rules
* Batteries must **only** be charged using designated, approved smart chargers (e.g., REV Slim Battery Chargers or smart balance chargers).
* Never leave batteries charging unattended overnight. The last person leaving the workshop must ensure all chargers are disconnected.
* Charge only on non-flammable surfaces (metal tables, fireproof battery bags, or ceramic tile).

### 2. Pre-Use Inspection
Before installing a battery on the robot, verify:
- [ ] No cuts, cracks, abrasions, or exposed copper on wires.
- [ ] Tamiya / XT30 connector housing is tight and uncharred.
- [ ] Battery casing exhibits no swelling, leaking, or physical deformation.
- [ ] Voltage tested with a battery analyzer (discard or quarantine batteries reading below minimum cell thresholds).

### 3. Emergency Battery Incident Protocol
If a battery becomes hot to the touch, swells, or emits smoke:
1. **Evacuate the immediate area** and alert a mentor immediately.
2. Do **not** touch bare metal or breathe fumes.
3. If safe, use insulated tongs to transfer the battery into the **Fireproof Battery Quarantine Bucket** filled with dry sand.
4. Keep a Class C / ABC fire extinguisher accessible within 10 meters of the charging station.

## 🧹 The "Zero-Janitor" Pack-Down & Station Ownership Protocol

A fundamental tenet of the RoboRangers is that **no member is ever treated as an unpaid cleaner for others**. Cleaning is not a punishment, nor is it delegated to juniors or the Sergeant-at-Arms. 

To eliminate the "team janitor" trap, the lab operates under **Decentralized Station Ownership**:

### 1. Zone Ownership Matrix
Every sub-team owns the pristine maintenance of their physical domain:
* **Mechanical Station (Chassis & Mechanisms Crew)**: Bits, sockets, wrenches returned to labeled shadow boards; aluminum shavings vacuumed; stock metal organized.
* **Electronics & Wiring Bench (Controls Crew)**: Heat shrink, wire strippers, XT30 connectors, and soldering irons cleaned, unplugged, and stowed in component drawers.
* **3D Printing & Filament Bay (3D Print Marshals)**: Scrap purge lines disposed of, print beds scraped clean with plastic scrapers, open filament spools returned to sealed dry-boxes.
* **Programming & CAD Desks (Software / Drivers)**: Laptops stowed, game controllers placed in charging docks, USB tethering cables coiled neatly.
* **Game Arena & Pit Floor (All Hands)**: Game elements returned to field starting positions, floor cleared of loose screws, zip-tie clippings, and tripping hazards.

### 2. The All-Hands 10-Minute Freeze
* **Synchronized Stop**: Exactly 10 minutes prior to adjournment, the Team Captain calls: *"Rangers, Pack-Down Freeze!"*
* All prototyping, coding, and robot driving **stops immediately**.
* Every member cleans and resets their own immediate station.
* **The Sergeant-at-Arms is an Auditor, NOT a Sweeper**: The Sergeant-at-Arms walks the room with an inspection clipboard. If a station is messy, the Sergeant-at-Arms **does not clean it**—they call back the specific sub-team members responsible to finish their zone.
* **The Lockout Penalty**: If a sub-team leaves their station disorganized, **their robot battery switch is tagged OUT**. That sub-team is locked out of live testing during the subsequent build session until their station passes audit.

## 🔑 Lab Access Control & RFID Keycard Regulations

To protect sensitive tooling, electronics, and proprietary robot systems while enabling productive autonomous work:

### 1. Keycard Custody & Eligibility
Lab RFID access cards are strictly privilege-based and issued only to:
1. **Official Mentors & Coaches**.
2. **RoboRangers Varsity Team Captain & Technical Mains**.
3. **Qualified Level 4 Varsity Members (Ages 16+)** who have demonstrated sustained maturity and possess verified Core/Vanguard rank with zero safety infractions.

### 2. Keycard Rules & Liability
* **Strictly Non-Transferable**: A keycard is issued to a specific individual. Lending, cloning, or handing off a keycard to another member or non-member results in **immediate permanent revocation** of card privileges.
* **Zero Tailgating & Cardholder Liability**: The cardholder who unlocks the lab door is **100% materially and legally responsible** for the lab and every person who enters with them.
* **Lost Cards**: Any lost or compromised card must be reported to Head Mentors within **2 hours** so the RFID identifier can be blacklisted in the electronic lock system.
* **Last Person Out Protocol**: The cardholder who leaves last must verify:
  - [ ] All soldering irons and heat guns are unplugged.
  - [ ] Battery charging banks are disconnected.
  - [ ] 3D printers left running have verified first-layer adhesion and camera monitoring.
  - [ ] All exterior windows are latched and lab door deadbolts are locked.

## 🌙 Extended Hours, Crunch Sprints & Overnight Regulations

During high-performance competition build cycles (FTC season crunch), extended lab sessions and overnight sprints are permitted **whenever needed**, subject to strict age, parental consent, and merit filters:

                                       ▲

 │ • Signed Paper Parental Consent on file for the entire active season        │
 │ • Merit Filter: Minimum verified XP & rank (eliminates casual hangers-on)   │
 │ • Eligible for extended evening build sessions and authorized overnight     │
 │   competition hackathons                                                    │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                                       ▲
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ TIER 3: Autonomous Senior Sprint Mode (Ages 16+ Leadership)                 │
 │ • Available to verified 16+ Varsity seniors, Mains, and Drive Team          │

 │ • Mandatory Buddy System: Never work alone (minimum 2 certified members)    │
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Mandatory Sprint Safeguards:
1. **Annual Signed Paper Parental Approval**: Prior to participating in any extended sprint or overnight session, a physical paper waiver must be signed by the student's legal guardian detailing emergency contacts, medical disclosures, and overnight transportation plans.
2. **The "Two-Person" Mandatory Safety Rule**: No individual may ever work in the lab alone. At all times, at least **two qualified individuals** must be present on-site.
3. **Heavy Machining Curfew (Fatigue Hazard Control)**:
   * **Past 22:00**, high-hazard rotary cutting tools, metal chop saws, and CNC routers are powered down.
   * Late-night hours are strictly reserved for low-hazard engineering: autonomous sensor path tuning, Java programming, CAD revisions, wiring/crimping, scouting analysis, and engineering notebook writing.
4. **Designated Rest Zone**: For overnight hackathons, the team provides a quiet rest room with clean sleeping mats and hydration, ensuring members can rest safely when fatigued.

## 📹 Workspace CCTV Security & Accountability Protocol

To safeguard team hardware, provide objective incident auditing, and ensure youth safety:

1. **Camera Placement**: Continuous recording CCTV cameras are installed covering all common workshop technical zones (machine area, 3D print farm, electronic benches, and arena field).
2. **Privacy & Exclusion**: Cameras are strictly prohibited in restrooms, changing rooms, and designated rest/sleep areas.
3. **Data Protection & GDPR Compliance**:
   * CCTV footage is stored securely on an encrypted local storage unit with a rolling auto-overwrite retention window (14–30 days).
   * Access to video feeds and recordings is strictly restricted to **Adult Head Mentors (Florin Cazac & Andrian Bocancea)**.
   * Footage is used solely for safety incident audits, material theft prevention, and post-accident analysis—never for public livestreaming without explicit separate consent.
4. **Parental Notice**: Continuous CCTV monitoring is formally disclosed in the annual membership enrollment agreement signed by parents and guardians.
