## 🛡️ Role Profiles, Responsibilities & Subordinate Crews

### 1. Team Captain (Squad President)
* **Scope**: Overall team director, culture keeper, standup facilitator, and mentor bridge.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Runs the **4-stage meeting rhythm**:
    1. *Kickoff Standup (5 mins)*: Goal setting and assigning tasks to the Mains.
    2. *Sprint Execution*: Monitoring progress and ensuring no subordinate is idling.
    3. *Testing / Mission Runs*: Gathering the squad to test attachments, code, or presentation scripts.
    4. *Ranger Pack-Down (10 mins)*: Supervising the Sergeant-at-Arms during end-of-meeting cleanup.
  * Serves as primary student spokesperson to mentors, tournament referees, and event judges.
  * Solves internal peer disputes using objective testing protocols.

---

### 2. PR Main (Public Relations & Portfolio Lead)
* **Scope**: Team branding, engineering notebook/portfolio curation, judging presentation, and public media.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Curates the official Engineering Portfolio / Innovation Display for competition judges.
  * Directs the judging presentation script, rehearsal timers, and flashcard drills.
  * Manages team social media, photography, match videos, and pit booth decoration.
* **Subordinates Under PR Main**:
  * *Portfolio / Notebook Writers*: Documenting daily engineering decisions, iterations, and calculations.
  * *Media & Pit Crew*: Taking photos/video footage and building the visual pit display.
  * *Presentation Speakers*: Practicing speaking roles and Q&A responses for the judging room.

---

### 3. Sergeant-at-Arms (Discipline, Safety, 3D Printers & Lab Marshal)
* **Scope**: Workshop order, meeting focus, 3D printers and filament inventory, tool organization, safety compliance, and lab hygiene.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * **3D Printers & Filament Stewardship (Primary Custodian)**: Directly responsible for the team's 3D printers and filament stock. Manages machine readiness, print queues, bed leveling, nozzle maintenance, and proper filament storage (keeping spools sealed, dry, categorized by material/color, and preventing waste).
  * Enforces 100% compliance with safety glasses in the workshop and pit areas.
  * Guardian of meeting focus: manages distractions, horseplay, and off-task behavior.
  * Enforces the non-negotiable rule: *"No cleanup, no robot testing."*
* **Subordinates Under Sergeant-at-Arms**:
  * *3D Printer & Filament Marshals*: Assist with print bed cleaning, monitoring active print jobs, loading/unloading spools, and maintaining filament dry-boxes.
  * *Tool & Hardware Keepers*: Ensure screws, hardware bins, Lego organizers, and hand tools are properly stored.
  * *Pit Cleanup Marshals*: Conduct end-of-session sweep-downs of tables, floors, and charging carts.
  * *Battery & Power Monitor*: Keeps track of battery cycle logs, charger statuses, and voltage safety checks.

---

### 4. Project Main (Sprint & Innovation Project Lead)
* **Scope**: Task timelines, milestone delivery, and research project coordination.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * **In FLL (TerraByte)**: Leads the real-world Innovation Project research, expert stakeholder interviews, and solution design.
  * **In Junior FTC / Varsity**: Acts as Agile Sprint Manager tracking CAD deadlines, part procurement, and task board cards.
* **Subordinates Under Project Main**:
  * *Research Squad*: Investigates problem statements, sources scientific data, and interviews industry experts.
  * *Prototype Assistants*: Fabricates physical models or props for the Innovation Project demonstration.
  * *Sprint Assistants*: Tracks checklist cards (To Do, In Progress, Done) on the team board.

---

### 5. Programming Main (Software & Controls Lead)
* **Scope**: Autonomous routines, tele-op driver controls, sensor telemetry, and codebase integrity.
* **Core Responsibilities**:
  * Architect of the squad's software (Block code / Python in FLL; Java / FTC SDK in FTC).
  * Manages code backups, Git commits, and sensor feedback loops (gyro, AprilTags, color sensors).
  * Tunes driver control sensitivity and gamepad button mappings in collaboration with drivers.
* **Subordinates Under Programming Main**:
  * *Apprentice Coders*: Learn basic syntax, write individual sensor logic routines, and document code comments.
  * *Sensor & Telemetry Testers*: Run consistency trials on the game mat or field perimeter, logging run success rates.
  * *Autonomous Checkers*: Verify field starting coordinates and camera alignment before matches.

## ⭐️ Leadership Points & Trust Credits

Taking on a board seat (Captain or Main) carries heightened accountability:
1. **Accelerated Advancement**: Serving a full season as a Main or Captain awards high **Leadership Points** in the student's Ranger Passport.
2. **Trust Credits**: Mains who successfully mentor and empower their subordinate members earn priority recommendation when vacancies open on the **RoboRangers Varsity Main Team**.
3. **Delegation Standard**: A Main who attempts to do all the work alone forfeits leadership points; excellence is measured by how well subordinates are trained and engaged.

---

## 🔀 Subordinate Direction Selection (Tracks)

Every member in **TerraByte** and **Junior FTC** must declare a **Primary Track** and join the subordinate crew of the corresponding Main:
1. **PR & Media Track** → Subordinate to PR Main
2. **Lab Safety & Operations Track** → Subordinate to Sergeant-at-Arms
3. **Innovation & Mechanical Design Track** → Subordinate to Project Main
4. **Programming & Autonomous Track** → Subordinate to Programming Main

## 🛡️ Non-Exploitation & Educational Benefit Rule (Child Labor Protection)

All roles, subordinate duties, and responsibilities outlined in this document must strictly adhere to the following non-negotiable principles:

1. **Strictly Educational & Mutual Benefit**:
   * All tasks and duties assigned to students must be actively analyzed and monitored to ensure they **never constitute the exploitation of child labor**.
   * The sole and absolute justification for all assigned roles is the **personal educational growth, technical skill acquisition, and empowerment of the individual student**, alongside the healthy development of the team as a youth organization.
   * Under no circumstances may students be treated as unpaid operational labor or used to replace commercial staff.

2. **Repayment of Work and Volunteering Hours**:
   * All hours of service, peer mentoring, lab upkeep, and subordinate work must be formally repaid to the student through:
     * **Team Status & Recognition**: Rank elevation within the RoboRangers hierarchy and trust credits.
     * **Future Progression**: Priority advancement for higher squad placement (TerraByte, Junior FTC, and Level 4 Varsity).
     * **Official Volunteering Certificates**: Verified service hour certificates (applicable for school graduation requirements, college admissions, and scholarship portfolios).
