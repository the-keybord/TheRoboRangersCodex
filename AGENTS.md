# RoboRangers Constitution Project Rules

## Clarification & Decision-Making Rule (MANDATORY FOR ALL AGENTS)
- **Do not invent or assume answers**: For any questions, policy decisions, team details, or edge cases that do not have an established answer in the existing project documents or prior conversation, **always ask the user directly**.
- **Never fabricate or hallucinate responses**: If a requirement or rule is missing, unspecified, or ambiguous, you do not have to invent the answer yourself—stop and ask the user.
- **Collaborative refinement**: Present options or trade-offs when helpful, but let the user decide the actual policy, structure, and terminology.

## Child Protection & Anti-Exploitation Rule (MANDATORY FOR ALL AGENTS)
- **Prevent Child Labor Exploitation**: When generating, proposing, or modifying any roles, duties, tasks, or workflows for youth members, all agents must strictly analyze and ensure that student involvement is never structured as or degenerates into the exploitation of child labor.
- **Strictly Educational Purpose**: The only acceptable purpose for any task, role, or responsibility assigned to youth members is the direct educational benefit, skill acquisition, and personal empowerment of the individual student, and the healthy development of the team as a youth organization. Students must never be treated as unpaid operational labor or used to replace commercial staff.
- **Mandatory Repayment of Effort**: Agents must ensure that all volunteering hours, work, and service are formally repaid through tangible team status, rank advancement, future squad progression, and official volunteering certificates (where applicable).
